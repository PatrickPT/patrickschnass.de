(https://openfontlicense.org). They are unmodified variable-font subsets (latin, latin-ext) as
served by Google Fonts, self-hosted so that no visitor data goes to Google.

To update: fetch `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=…` with a current browser user agent,
download the `latin` and `latin-ext` files and adjust `fonts.css`.
